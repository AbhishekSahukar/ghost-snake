Controls

  • ↑↓←→ or WASD steer
  • Space / Enter start or resume
  • P pause   R restart   M mute

How to play

  • Eat pellets to grow and score 10.
  • The glowing power pellet turns every ghost blue for 5 seconds.
  • Blue ghosts are safe to eat: 200, 400, 800, 1600 in a row.
  • Coloured ghosts are deadly. So are walls and your own tail.
